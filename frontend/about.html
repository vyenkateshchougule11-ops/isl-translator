How it works

A short tour of what happens between your camera and the translated sentence, plus tips for getting the best results.

From camera to sentence

Six stages run on your own computer for every frame.

1

Capture

The Python backend reads the webcam directly and mirrors each frame, exactly as it did while the model was trained.

2

Find landmarks

MediaPipe Holistic locates your upper body and both hands. Your face gets a blue box and your hands get yellow boxes.

3

Make features

Landmarks become 258 numbers per frame, measured from your shoulders so distance from the camera does not matter.

4

Recognise

A three-layer LSTM network looks at the latest 60 frames (about 3 seconds) and scores every word.

5

Stabilise

A word is accepted only with at least 70% confidence and when it wins 4 of the last 8 votes. Resting hands are ignored.

6

Present

Words join a sentence you can edit. Show it, speak it, or share a read-only link with another person.

Tips for best results

Small things that make recognition noticeably better.

💡

Good light

Face a window or lamp. Avoid strong light behind you.

🖼️

Plain background

A calm background helps the camera find your hands.

🙆

Show upper body

Keep both shoulders and both hands inside the picture.

📏

Sit about an arm's length away

Not too close, not too far. Stay roughly where you were when recording.

⏱️

Sign calmly

Do one sign, then rest your hands for a moment before the next.

🧹

Fix mistakes quickly

Click × on a wrong word or drag words into a better order before completing the sentence.

Questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. The camera is processed on your own computer. Only if you press Share live view does a read-only copy of the video and translation travel through a temporary link, and it stops as soon as you stop sharing.

Why does the first word take about three seconds?

The model needs a full window of 60 frames (3 seconds) before it can make its first decision. After that it updates continuously.

Why did a word appear that I did not sign?

Hand movement between two signs can look like the start of a sign. Remove the word with its × button. Sign calmly and pause between words to reduce this.

Can more words be added?

Yes. Record and train again in the notebook, then copy the new model and metadata files into the models folder. The app reads the word list from the metadata automatically.

What happens when I leave the Translator page?

The camera is released. Completed sentences and an active share link are kept, so you can come back and continue.
