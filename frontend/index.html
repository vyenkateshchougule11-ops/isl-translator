How it works

Four simple steps from a camera frame to a spoken sentence.

1

See

The camera is read on your computer. A blue box marks your face and yellow boxes mark your hands.

2

Track

MediaPipe finds your body and hand landmarks and turns them into 258 numbers per frame.

3

Recognise

An LSTM network reads the last 3 seconds and names the word you signed.

4

Speak

Words form a sentence you can fix, then show as text, play as audio, or share by link.
